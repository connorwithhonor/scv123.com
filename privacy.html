Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

SCV123.com (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by Connor MacIvor in connection with real estate services in the Santa Clarita Valley, California. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use this website, text our published phone number, or otherwise contact us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and property address when you submit a form on this site
  • Phone number, opt-in keyword, message content, and timestamp when you text any of our published keywords (e.g., BRIDGEPORT, VALUE, CALL) to 661-263-4801
  • Any messages or notes you include in form submissions or text replies (e.g., timeline, goals, questions, property address)
  • Information you send by email or other communication in connection with a potential or actual listing or transaction

We may also automatically collect certain technical data when you visit the site, such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. We may use cookies or similar technologies to improve site function and analytics.

SMS Data Handling

How our SMS service works

When you opt in to our SMS service by texting one of our published keywords to 661-263-4801, we collect and store your phone number, the keyword you texted, the date and time of your message, and any subsequent reply content for the purpose of providing the requested real estate information and ongoing market updates.

We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS data is stored in our customer relationship management system (HonorElevate, a white-labeled GoHighLevel instance operated under our subscription) and is protected under our standard data security practices.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP, after which we will retain only the opt-out record for compliance purposes and will not send you further messages. You can also text HELP for assistance.

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your wireless carrier.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries (whether by form, email, voice call, or SMS) and schedule or conduct seller strategy calls
  • Provide real estate services and communicate about listings, transactions, market updates, and related matters
  • Send follow-up communications you have requested or that are reasonably related to your inquiry, including ongoing market updates if you have opted into our nurture cadence
  • Improve our website, services, and marketing (including aggregated or anonymized analytics)
  • Comply with legal or regulatory obligations, including TCPA and CTIA SMS compliance requirements

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the site or business (e.g., hosting, email, CRM, SMS routing, scheduling tools), subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Other parties as needed to perform real estate services (e.g., brokerage, title, escrow, lender, MLS) or as required by law

Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in those emails or by contacting us. You may opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP to any of our text messages. You can disable or limit cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality.

Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to comply with legal or professional obligations (including real estate record-keeping requirements), and to resolve disputes. SMS opt-out records are retained for compliance purposes even after you opt out.

California and Other Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the CCPA or other laws (e.g., access, deletion, opt-out of sale). We do not sell personal information. To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the information below.
